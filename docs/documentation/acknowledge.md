# Acknowledge PICLas

Please acknowledge the role that PICLas played in your research.

## In presentations

For your presentations, you can find a PICLas acknowledgment slide [here](piclas_presentation.png). Feel free to use it to acknowledge PICLas in your presentations.

## Acknowledge Sentence

Please add the following acknowledgment sentence to your publications.

`````{tab-set}
````{tab-item} Plain text
This research used the open-source code [PICLas](https://github.com/piclas-framework/piclas).
Primary PICLas contributors are the Institute of Space Systems at the University of Stuttgart and boltzplatz - numerical plasma dynamics GmbH.
We acknowledge all PICLas contributors.
````
````{tab-item} LaTex
```latex
\usepackage{hyperref}
This research used the open-source code \href{https://github.com/piclas-framework/piclas}{PICLas}. Primary PICLas contributors are the Institute of Space Systems at the University of Stuttgart and boltzplatz - numerical plasma dynamics GmbH. We acknowledge all PICLas contributors.
```
`````
